SCOPE & PROVENANCE

A planning aid, not a claim of ground truth.

The workspace uses project-supplied GIS records and selected processed environmental summaries. It is not a live monitoring feed or an authoritative municipal register. Source coverage, coordinate completeness and satellite observation dates vary; record counts alone do not prove a service gap or a community’s need.

Read each layer with its source, study extent, method and limitations.
